# FoodHub Promotions, Coupons and Compensation Policy

This policy explains how coupons and credits work and how FoodHub may compensate customers when something goes wrong.

## Overview
FoodHub offers promotional coupons to attract and thank customers. It may also give goodwill coupons or credits when an order goes wrong. Compensation is always decided by a human support agent and the chatbot cannot promise it.

## Coupons

### How coupons work
A coupon gives a discount on an order and is applied at checkout. Each coupon has its own terms, such as a minimum order value, a maximum discount, the valid period and the restaurants where it can be used.

### Limits
Coupons cannot be exchanged for cash. Unless the terms say otherwise, only one coupon can be used per order. Expired coupons cannot be restored.

## Coupons on cancelled or refunded orders
If an order is cancelled in time, a coupon used on it is usually returned to the customer if it has not expired. If part of an order is refunded, the discount is adjusted in proportion to the refunded items. A coupon used on a delivered order is not restored.

## Compensation when something goes wrong

### Delayed orders
When an order is late by more than 30 minutes, a support agent may offer a goodwill coupon or a partial refund. The amount depends on the delay and the order value and is decided case by case.

### Service problems
For other service problems, such as repeated mistakes or poor handling, an agent may offer credit or a coupon. Compensation is a goodwill gesture and does not replace a refund that is owed under the Refund Policy.

### What the chatbot can say
The chatbot can explain that compensation exists and that an agent decides it. It cannot say how much will be given or promise that any will be given.

## Wallet credit
Some refunds and goodwill gestures are given as wallet credit. Wallet credit can be used on future orders and has an expiry date shown in the app. It cannot be withdrawn as cash unless a support agent approves a bank transfer for an approved refund.

## Fair use
Coupons and compensation are meant for genuine use. Misuse, such as creating multiple accounts for discounts or false refund claims, may lead to coupons being cancelled and the account being reviewed.

## Frequently asked questions
- Can the chatbot give me a coupon for a late order? No, a support agent decides compensation.
- Do I get my coupon back if I cancel? Usually yes, if the order was cancelled in time and the coupon has not expired.
- Can I turn coupons into cash? No.
