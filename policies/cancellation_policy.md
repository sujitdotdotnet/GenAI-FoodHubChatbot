# FoodHub Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when a FoodHub order can be cancelled, what fees may apply, and how a cancellation request is handled. FoodHub order statuses are: preparing food, picked up, delivered and canceled. The status shown on the order decides what is possible.

## Overview
FoodHub cancels orders fairly: the earlier a customer asks, the more likely the cancellation is free. Food is made fresh for each order, so once the restaurant has started cooking, the cost of that food is real, and a fee may apply. Once the delivery partner has the food, it cannot be recalled.

The chatbot cannot cancel an order by itself. It checks the order status, explains what the policy allows, and passes the request to a human support agent, who confirms the outcome. The chatbot never promises that a cancellation or refund will be approved.

## Cancelling by order status

### Status "preparing food"
While the status is "preparing food", the restaurant has accepted the order and is cooking it. The order can only be cancelled with the restaurant's approval. A cancellation fee of up to 50% of the food value may apply because the food has already been made. If the order has just entered this status and the preparation has not really begun, the agent can often waive the fee. The customer should ask as early as possible.

### Status "picked up"
When the status is "picked up", the delivery partner has collected the order and is on the way. At this stage the order can no longer be cancelled. If the customer no longer wants the order, they may refuse it at the door, but a refund is not guaranteed. If there is a problem with the food after it arrives, the Refund Policy applies and the issue must be reported within 24 hours.

### Status "delivered"
A "delivered" order has been handed over to the customer and cannot be cancelled. If something was wrong with the order (missing item, wrong item, quality or food-safety problem), the customer can request a refund under the Refund Policy within 24 hours of delivery.

### Status "canceled"
An order whose status is "canceled" has already been cancelled. Nothing more needs to be done. Orders cancelled in time are not charged, and any amount paid online is returned to the original payment method. For cash-on-delivery (COD) orders there is nothing to refund because no money was collected.

## Cancellation fees

### How the fee is calculated
The fee is based on the food value only (not taxes or delivery charges) and depends on how far the restaurant has progressed. It is never more than 50% of the food value. The support agent confirms the exact amount before the cancellation is finalised, and the customer can decide whether to continue.

### When the fee is waived
The fee is waived when the restaurant takes more than 15 minutes beyond the preparation ETA, when the restaurant itself cancels or cannot fulfil the order, when an item is out of stock, or when the problem was caused by FoodHub (for example a wrong price or a technical error).

## Restaurant-initiated and FoodHub-initiated cancellations
Sometimes the restaurant or FoodHub has to cancel an order, for instance when the kitchen closes early or the delivery partner cannot reach the address. In that case the order is marked "canceled", the customer pays nothing, and any online payment is refunded in full. FoodHub may offer a goodwill coupon, decided by the support agent.

## Cash on delivery (COD) orders
COD orders have no online payment, so a cancelled COD order creates no refund. A repeated pattern of COD orders being cancelled after the food has been prepared, or refused at the door, may lead FoodHub to require prepayment on future orders.

## How to request a cancellation

### Using the chatbot
The customer selects the Order ID and says they want to cancel. The chatbot looks at the order status, explains the policy for that status and forwards the request to a human support agent.

### What the support agent checks
The agent checks the current status, the preparation and delivery ETAs, whether any fee applies and whether it should be waived, and how the order was paid. The agent then confirms to the customer whether the cancellation is approved and what refund or fee applies.

## Frequently asked questions
- Can I cancel after the restaurant has started cooking? Only with the restaurant's approval, and a fee of up to 50% of the food value may apply.
- Can I cancel when the order is picked up? No. The order is already on its way.
- Can I cancel a delivered order? No. Delivered orders can only be handled through a refund request if something was wrong.
- Will I be charged for an order that is already canceled? No. A canceled order is not charged.
- How long does a refund for a cancelled prepaid order take? See the Refund Policy for timelines by payment method.
