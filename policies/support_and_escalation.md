# FoodHub Support and Escalation Policy

This policy explains what the FoodHub chatbot can and cannot do, when a conversation is passed to a human agent, and what customers can expect afterwards.

## Overview
The chatbot answers questions about one customer order using the order record and FoodHub's policy documents. It is designed to be helpful, accurate and honest. When it cannot verify something or when a decision is needed, it hands the conversation to a human support agent.

## What the chatbot can do
The chatbot can tell the customer the status of their order, the preparation and delivery ETAs, the items, and the payment status. It can explain FoodHub's cancellation, refund, delivery and payment policies and say which one applies to the order. It can pass requests to a human agent.

## What the chatbot cannot do
The chatbot cannot cancel orders, issue refunds, change addresses, give compensation or promise any outcome. It cannot talk about other customers or other orders. It cannot confirm ingredients or allergens. These actions need a human agent or the restaurant.

## When the conversation goes to a human agent

### Requests that need a decision
Cancellations, refunds, compensation, address changes and disputes are always passed to a human agent, who confirms the outcome.

### Repeated or unresolved complaints
If a customer says they have raised the same problem several times without a resolution, the chatbot escalates the conversation to a human agent with priority.

### Frustration or urgency
If the customer is upset, angry or in a hurry, the chatbot apologises and routes the request to a support specialist. It does not argue and it does not make promises it cannot keep.

### Information the chatbot cannot verify
When the order record or the policies do not contain the answer, the chatbot says so honestly and offers a human agent instead of guessing.

## What happens after a handover
A support agent reviews the order and the conversation, contacts the customer and confirms the outcome. The chatbot does not promise a specific outcome or a specific time. Urgent cases, such as a food-safety issue or an order that has not arrived long after its ETA, are handled first.

## What the agent needs
To help quickly, the customer should have the Order ID ready and describe the problem clearly. For quality or wrong-item problems, a photo helps. The agent never asks for card numbers, CVV, PINs or OTPs.

## Respectful conduct
FoodHub staff, restaurants and delivery partners are treated with respect. Abusive language may lead to the conversation being closed and the account being reviewed.

## Frequently asked questions
- Can the chatbot cancel my order? No, it passes the request to a human agent who confirms the outcome.
- What if the chatbot does not know the answer? It says so and offers a human agent.
- Will an agent contact me? Yes, after a handover a support agent reviews the case and confirms the outcome.
