# FoodHub Payment FAQ

Answers to common questions about paying for FoodHub orders, payment statuses, failed payments and payment safety.

## Payment status values
Every order shows a payment status. "COD" means cash on delivery: the customer pays the delivery partner when the order arrives, and no money has been collected yet. "completed" means the online payment was received successfully. "canceled" means the order was cancelled and no payment is due, or any paid amount is being returned. The payment status and the order status are separate: for example an order can be "picked up" with a "COD" payment status.

## Accepted payment methods
FoodHub accepts credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets and cash on delivery in supported areas. Some restaurants or areas may not offer cash on delivery. The available methods are shown at checkout.

## Cash on delivery (COD)

### How COD works
The customer pays the exact amount to the delivery partner at the door. Where possible, the customer should keep the correct change ready because delivery partners may not carry much cash.

### Cancelling a COD order
A COD order that is cancelled before pickup has no charge, and no refund is needed because no payment was taken. COD orders that are repeatedly refused at the door may lead to prepayment being required later.

## Failed or double payments

### Money deducted but no order
If money was deducted from the customer's account but the order was not placed, the amount is automatically returned within 5 to 7 working days. The customer does not need to do anything, but can ask a support agent to check if it takes longer.

### Charged twice for one order
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, a support agent can verify both transactions and raise a correction. The extra amount is returned to the original payment method.

### Payment pending
If the payment shows as pending, the customer should wait a few minutes before paying again, so that they are not charged twice. If the pending state stays, a support agent can check it.

## Payment safety
FoodHub support and the chatbot never ask for a full card number, CVV, PIN, UPI PIN, net-banking password or one-time password (OTP) in the chat. Customers should never share these details. Anyone who asks for them is not FoodHub support.

## Invoices and prices
The final amount includes the food price, taxes, delivery charge and any platform fee, minus discounts. An invoice is available for completed orders. If a customer believes the price is wrong, a support agent reviews the invoice and corrects any error found.

## Tips
Tips go to the delivery partner. A tip is refunded only when the order was not delivered. Tips are not refunded when the order was delivered with a minor problem that is refunded separately.

## Refunds
All refunds go back to the original payment method and are reviewed by a human support agent. Timelines are in the Refund Policy.

## Frequently asked questions
- What does a payment status of COD mean? You pay in cash on delivery, and nothing has been charged yet.
- I was charged but my order failed. What now? The amount is returned automatically within 5 to 7 working days.
- Is it safe to share my card details with the chatbot? No. Never share card numbers, CVV, PINs or OTPs.
