# FoodHub Refund Policy

This policy describes when FoodHub refunds an order, how to ask for a refund, how much is refunded and how long it takes. Every refund request is reviewed by a human support agent. The chatbot can explain the policy but cannot approve or issue a refund.

## Overview
FoodHub refunds money when the customer paid for something they did not receive or when the food was not safe or not as described. Refunds are not given simply because the customer changed their mind after delivery. Refunds go back to the original payment method unless the order was paid in cash.

## When a refund is possible

### Order cancelled in time
If an order is cancelled before the restaurant starts preparing it, and the customer paid online, the full amount is refunded. If a cancellation fee applies, the fee is deducted from the refund. See the Cancellation Policy for details.

### Missing item
If an item that was part of the order is missing from the delivery, FoodHub refunds the price of that item. The customer should report it within 24 hours of delivery.

### Wrong item
If the customer received an item they did not order, FoodHub refunds the item or the order value, depending on whether the delivered food is usable. The agent decides after reviewing the photo and the order.

### Quality or food-safety problem
Food that arrived spoiled, undercooked, contaminated, or with a foreign object is eligible for a full refund of the affected items. For safety reasons the customer should stop eating the food and keep it, or a photo of it, for review.

### Order not delivered
If the order was marked delivered but never arrived, or the order was never delivered at all, the customer is refunded in full after the agent checks the delivery record.

### Very late delivery
If an order arrives more than 30 minutes after the estimated delivery time, the customer can ask a support agent to review it. A partial refund or a goodwill coupon may be offered, decided case by case.

## When a refund is not given
Refunds are not given for a change of mind after delivery, for personal taste preferences, for incorrect addresses entered by the customer, or when the customer could not be reached for delivery after several attempts. A refund may also be refused if the issue is reported after the 24-hour window.

## How to request a refund

### The 24-hour rule
A refund request must be raised within 24 hours of the delivery time. Requests after that period are reviewed only in exceptional cases.

### What to provide
The customer should give the Order ID, describe what was wrong, and share a clear photo when the problem is visible (for example a missing or wrong item, or damaged food). The agent may ask follow-up questions.

## Refund amounts
The refund is the value of the affected items and, when the whole order failed, the order value including delivery charges. Taxes follow the refunded items. Coupons used on the order are adjusted proportionally. Tips are refunded only when the order was not delivered.

## Refund timelines
Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. Card and net-banking refunds usually take 5 to 7 working days. UPI refunds usually take 1 to 3 working days. Wallet refunds usually arrive within 24 hours. The time shown here starts when the refund is approved, and banks may take longer to show it on a statement.

## Refunds for cash on delivery (COD) orders
No money is refunded to a COD order that was cancelled before delivery, because nothing was paid. If a COD order was delivered and then approved for a refund, the amount is returned as wallet credit or by bank transfer after the customer verifies their payout details with a support agent.

## Disputes and review
If the customer disagrees with a refund decision, they can ask for a second review. A different support agent reviews the case. The decision after the second review is final.

## Frequently asked questions
- How long do I have to ask for a refund? 24 hours after delivery.
- How long does a card refund take? Usually 5 to 7 working days after approval.
- Can the chatbot refund me? No. A human agent reviews and approves every refund.
- Do I get a refund if I simply do not like the taste? No, unless the food was unsafe or clearly not as described.
