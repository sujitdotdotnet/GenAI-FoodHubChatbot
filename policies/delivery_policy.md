# FoodHub Delivery Policy

This policy explains how FoodHub estimates and delivers orders, what happens when an order is late, and what customers can do about delivery problems.

## Overview
Every order shows an estimated delivery time. FoodHub works with independent delivery partners who collect the order from the restaurant and bring it to the customer's address. The estimate depends on the restaurant's preparation time, the distance, traffic and weather.

## Delivery estimates

### How the estimate is made
The preparation ETA is the time the restaurant expects to finish cooking. The delivery ETA is the time the order is expected to reach the customer after pickup. An order only gets a delivery ETA once the food has been prepared and a delivery partner has taken it, so an order that is still being prepared normally has no delivery ETA yet.

### Why the estimate can change
Heavy traffic, bad weather, a busy kitchen or a long delivery distance can move the time. FoodHub updates the estimate when it can, but the time shown is an estimate and not a guarantee.

## Delayed orders

### When an order counts as delayed
An order is considered delayed when it arrives more than 30 minutes after the estimated delivery time, or when it is still being prepared more than 15 minutes after the preparation ETA.

### What the customer can do
The customer can ask a support agent to look into a delayed order. The agent checks where the order is and talks to the restaurant or the delivery partner.

### Compensation
Compensation for a delay, such as a goodwill coupon or a partial refund, is decided by the support agent case by case. The chatbot cannot promise compensation.

## Order tracking
Once the order status is "picked up", the delivery partner is on the way to the customer. The delivery partner may call the customer to confirm the location. Customers should keep their phone reachable until the order arrives.

## Delivery attempts and unreachable customers
The delivery partner tries to reach the customer by phone. If the customer cannot be reached after several attempts, or nobody is available at the address, the order may be marked undelivered. An order that fails for these reasons may not be eligible for a refund.

## Changing the delivery address

### Before pickup
The address can be changed only before the order is picked up, and only through a human support agent. The agent checks whether the new address is within the delivery area and whether the delivery time or charge changes.

### After pickup
Once the order is picked up, the address cannot be changed.

## Delivery area and charges
FoodHub delivers within the service area of each restaurant. Delivery charges depend on the distance and demand and are shown at checkout. Delivery charges are refunded only when the whole order fails.

## Contactless delivery and proof of delivery
The customer can ask for contactless delivery. The delivery partner leaves the order at the door and marks it delivered. FoodHub records the delivery time as proof of delivery. If a customer says an order marked delivered never arrived, a support agent reviews the delivery record under the Refund Policy.

## Frequently asked questions
- Why does my order have no delivery ETA? The food is probably still being prepared; the delivery ETA appears after pickup.
- What if my order is very late? Ask a support agent to review it; compensation is decided case by case.
- Can I change my address? Only before pickup and only through a human agent.
