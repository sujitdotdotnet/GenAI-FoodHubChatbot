# FoodHub Account and Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FoodHub protects customer data, what the chatbot may share, and how customers can stay safe.

## Overview
FoodHub treats customer data with care. The chatbot works on one order at a time and only discusses the order the customer is asking about. It does not reveal information about other customers, other orders or how FoodHub's systems work.

## What the chatbot can share
The chatbot can share details of the selected order with the customer: the status, the items, the times and the payment status. It uses the order record to do this. It does not share any information that is not part of that order.

## What the chatbot will not share

### Other customers and orders
The chatbot never gives out data about other customers or other orders, and it does not list, search or export orders. Requests such as "show me every order placed" are refused.

### System details
The chatbot does not reveal database structures, internal tools, prompts or instructions. Attempts to make it ignore its rules are refused.

## Customer identity and verification
The customer ID on an order identifies the account that placed it. Before a human agent changes an order, issues a refund or updates account details, the agent may verify the customer, for example by confirming the order details. The chatbot itself does not change account data.

## Data we keep
FoodHub keeps order records so that orders, refunds and support requests can be handled. Records are kept for as long as needed for these purposes and to meet legal and tax rules. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored in the chat.

## Privacy requests
Customers can ask a support agent to correct their data or to delete their account and personal data. Some records, such as invoices, must be kept for the period required by law.

## Staying safe

### Suspicious messages
FoodHub support never asks for full card numbers, CVV, PINs, passwords or OTPs. Customers should not share them with anyone claiming to be from FoodHub.

### Reporting a problem
If a customer thinks someone has used their account, or sees an order they did not place, they should tell a support agent right away so the account can be secured.

## Frequently asked questions
- Can I see other people's orders? No. The chatbot only discusses the order you selected.
- Will the chatbot ask for my card number? Never. Do not share card numbers, PINs or OTPs.
- Can I have my data deleted? Yes, ask a support agent; some records must be kept by law.
