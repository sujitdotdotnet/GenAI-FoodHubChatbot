# FoodHub Food Safety and Quality Policy

This policy explains FoodHub's standards for food safety and quality, what customers should do when there is a problem, and what the chatbot can and cannot say about ingredients and allergens.

## Overview
FoodHub works with restaurant partners who must follow local food-safety laws. Safety comes first: if food seems unsafe, the customer should not eat it. Quality problems are handled through the Refund Policy.

## Restaurant partner standards
Partner restaurants must keep their kitchens clean, store ingredients at the right temperature, use fresh ingredients and pack food in clean, sealed containers. FoodHub may remove restaurants that repeatedly fail these standards.

## Packaging and delivery
Orders are packed in sealed bags or tamper-evident containers. Hot food should arrive warm and cold food should arrive cool. If the seal is broken when the order arrives, the customer should not accept the order or should report it immediately.

## Allergens and dietary needs

### What the chatbot can say
The chatbot cannot confirm the ingredients of any dish or whether a dish is free of an allergen. Ingredient information comes from the restaurant and may be incomplete or out of date.

### What customers should do
Customers with allergies or strict dietary needs should contact the restaurant directly before ordering and should not rely on the chatbot. If a customer has a reaction after eating, they should seek medical help immediately and then contact FoodHub support.

## Quality problems

### Examples
Examples of quality problems are food that is cold when it should be hot, undercooked or burnt food, stale or spoiled food, wrong portions, and food that is clearly not what was described.

### What to do
Report the problem within 24 hours of delivery with the Order ID and a clear photo. A support agent reviews the case. Eligible problems are refunded under the Refund Policy.

## Food-safety incidents

### Foreign objects and contamination
If the customer finds a foreign object, insects, mould or signs of contamination, they should stop eating, keep the food or take photos, and report it right away. These cases are treated as urgent, and the affected items are fully refunded after review.

### Illness after a meal
If a customer becomes unwell after a meal, they should get medical help first. FoodHub support will then review the order and investigate the restaurant.

## Frequently asked questions
- Can the chatbot tell me if a dish contains nuts? No. Please contact the restaurant directly.
- What if my food arrives cold? Report it within 24 hours with a photo so an agent can review it.
- I found something in my food. What should I do? Stop eating, keep the evidence and report it immediately.
